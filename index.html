About

Houston Raised. Global Scholar. Future Executive.

My goal is to become an impactful Chief Revenue Officer and one day establish my own endowment for low-income students.

I'm a junior at the University of Houston's C.T. Bauer College of Business, studying Marketing and Supply Chain Management with a 4.0 GPA. I'm also in the Program for Excellence in Selling at the Stephen Stagner Sales Excellence Institute, the nation's premier sales program, where I'm earning a minor in Professional Sales and made President's Club.

I turn customer insight into stories that move people to act. This summer at Aramco, I worked on events and branding for the FIFA Fan Festival Houston, CERAWeek, and the Aramco Houston Half Marathon, which together reached 1.2 million brand touchpoints. I also led an internal audit of Public Affairs' impact and proposed a company-wide chargeback model, which earned me the NextGen Leaders MVP Award.

Competing pushes me. I represented UH as a Case Competition Fellow at the HSI Battle of the Brains, where my team designed an AI shopping browser extension and placed 2nd nationally in the business component, and where The Home Depot named me one of 2 Orange Hearted Scholars out of 122 competitors. I also led marketing strategy for our 2nd-place team at the UH x Phillips 66 Global Case Competition.

I'm a 2026 ELC Lowe's Marvin R. Ellison Scholar, a Management Leadership for Tomorrow Career Prep Fellow, a HACU Voces Nuevas Campus Ambassador, and the Undergraduate Student Representative on the Bauer College Advisory Board. Before UH, I earned my associate's degree cum laude at College of the Mainland, and in high school I ran my own brownie business that raised $20,000.

Outside of school and work, you'll find me reading, journaling, taking photos, lifting, playing volleyball, or adding to my CD collection.

    What do the colors mean?

    Each of the 34 CliftonStrengths fits into one of four domains. The domains describe how a strength helps you execute, influence others, build relationships, and absorb and think about information.

    • Executing themes help you make things happen.
    • Influencing themes help you take charge, speak up, and make sure others are heard.
    • Relationship Building themes help you build strong relationships that hold a team together.
    • Strategic Thinking themes help you absorb and analyze information that informs better decisions.
